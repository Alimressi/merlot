What this place is

A restaurant that opens with the city

There are two words in the name and both are true. Wine — a list of Azerbaijani wines and a house wine in three colours: red, white, rosé. Dine — a kitchen that starts at eight in the morning: breakfast, lunch and dinner in the same room.

The room is small and quiet: a brick arch, blue velvet, wood, and light that is turned down towards the evening. The reviews mostly call it cosy, and people come back.

Wine

House wine in three colours

It comes up in the reviews more than anything else — red, white and rosé, and guests tell you to try all three. Beside it, a list of Azerbaijani wines; the red arrives with a decanter.

A glass here is not strictly an evening thing. Some come for coffee and stay for a glass, some the other way round — the room opens at eight, the rest is up to you.

Evenings

Towards the night it gets quieter and darker

The lights come down, the bar starts mixing, and on special evenings there is live music — guitar and clarinet under the brick arch. The restaurant announces those dates on Instagram.
